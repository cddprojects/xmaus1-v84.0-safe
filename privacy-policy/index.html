Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Roleblend Australia (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Who we are

Roleblend Australia, Pitt Street, Haymarket NSW 2000. Email: info@roleblend.com.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what we need to run this website and answer your questions:

  • Information you give us. When you use our enquiry form, your name, email address and the question you write.
  • Information collected automatically. Standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited and the time of your visit, which is typically recorded in server logs.

Please don’t include sensitive information (such as health details, tax file numbers or bank details) in your question. We don’t need it.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you submit the form or email us, and automatically through your use of the site.

4. Why we collect and use it

  • To read and reply to your enquiry.
  • To keep the website secure, reliable and easy to use.
  • To understand, in general terms, how the guide is used so we can improve it.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use your email address for marketing unless you have asked us to.

5. Who we share it with

We may share information with service providers who help us run the site, such as web hosting, email and form-handling providers. They may only use it to provide services to us. We may also disclose information if required or authorised by law.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of our providers may store or process information outside Australia. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure the provider handles your information consistently with the APPs. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads.

7. Cookies and analytics

This site doesn’t currently set advertising cookies. If we add analytics or similar tools, we will update this policy and, where appropriate, ask for your consent. You can adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies.

8. Links to other websites

Our pages link to official sources such as the Fair Work Ombudsman, the Australian Business Register and business.gov.au. We aren’t responsible for the privacy practices of other sites, so please read their policies.

9. How we keep it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to respond and meet our obligations, then securely delete or de-identify them.

10. Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing info@roleblend.com. We’ll respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

11. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

12. Complaints

If you think we’ve breached the APPs, please contact us at info@roleblend.com with the details. We’ll investigate and reply in writing. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the OAIC at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
